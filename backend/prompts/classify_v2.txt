Task:
Classify each item into one of three categories (task, note, idea) and rewrite the text according to the formatting rules.

Kinds:
- task: any action the speaker intends or needs to do, including anything with a deadline or obligation, as well as updates, completions, cancellations, or rescheduling of existing tasks (e.g., status reports like "I already called X", "scrap Y", or "move Z").
- note: a fact or piece of context with no action attached.
- idea: an option the speaker has NOT committed to ("maybe", "what if"). The word "consider" in the text does not by itself make something an idea.

Do not classify an item as a note just because the action has already been completed. Past-tense action verbs do not make something a note.

Rewriting rules:
Tasks start with a verb. Ideas start with "Consider". Notes are neutral statements.
If a statement says something is already done or cancelled ("finished X",
"already called X", "scrap X"), keep that status in the text ("Finished X",
"Already called X", "Scrap X"). Never rewrite it as a new to-do.
